# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SaaS and internal operations teams managing users and reviewing business activity.

## Product Purpose

A reusable Ant Design admin starter with runnable local demonstrations of common workflows.

## Brand Personality

Modern, clean, professional. Follow the supplied `design/adminpro-design-docs/` and screenshots.

## Anti-references

Avoid excessive outlines, heavy shadows, arbitrary status colors, and custom replacements for standard Ant Design controls.

## Design Principles

- Make data, state, and actions immediately understandable.
- Reuse Ant Design and Pro Components interaction patterns.
- Configure identity once and propagate it throughout the application.
- Keep all five documented pages usable on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Clearly distinguish working local demonstrations from backend-dependent operations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain keyboard operation, visible focus, readable text, reduced motion, and accessible chart data tables.
